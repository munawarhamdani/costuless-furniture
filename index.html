Get to know us

Furniture that makes every space feel right.

Cost U Less Furniture is a Gaborone-based retail furniture provider, established in 1996. We specialise in delivering high-quality, modern and affordable furniture solutions for residential and professional environments.

From homes and start-ups to established businesses and public institutions, we help customers furnish their spaces without compromising on durability or value.

Our vision

To be Botswana's trusted destination for affordable living and working solutions.

We aim to transform spaces through modern design, exceptional value and accessible furniture for every budget. We believe comfort and durability should be within reach.
